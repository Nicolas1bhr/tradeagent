**Choices, all written into `CONTRACTS.md` at `U-flatten-3` and restated here.** A `VALUATION_LOST` exit does NOT close the day and is NOT a strike: nobody has measured a loss, so filing it as a breach would claim a budget was reached; the instrument stays refused new risk only while it cannot be valued. **Deviation from the brief's key:** the exit is keyed by the EPISODE — `loss_valuation_exit:{connector}:{account}:{symbol}:{yyyyMMddTHHmmssZ}` — not `{utcDay}`, because two silences on one instrument on one date are two events and a day key would make the second one's write-once insert answer false and leave a position nothing would ever close. Zero on `ValuationLossExitMinutes` is OFF and is the WIDEST value (every other zero in `RiskPolicy` reads that way), so LENGTHENING is the two-press widening through `Widenings`; 15 minutes out of the box, a judgment; `GatewayOptions.ValuationLossExitAfter` is the fallback when the settings row cannot be read. Two new app press kinds (`op-valuation-cancel-`, `op-valuation-close-`) and a separate boundary kind, so no surface dates a valuation exit by a budget and no exit can open the boundary a loss episode's extension names. The episode row is an upsert the watch refreshes under the dispatch gate while the EXIT is write-once at the SQL layer. The precautionary cancel takes only orders that could INCREASE exposure — a resting protective order is the one thing bounding an unmeasurable position — runs ONCE per episode, and clears the flags it wrote behind its own read-back, or a data outage would pause the whole product.
