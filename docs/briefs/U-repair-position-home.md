## Protections touched, and how each is kept
- No terminal: the repair is an in-app press with its result in words naming both folders. Operator authority: the act is in-process only — no `trade` verb, no pipe op, no board act reaches it (a test over the pipe's op table and the verb list). It moves no money and widens no limit.
- Never destroy without the owner's choice: nothing is deleted; the refused home is MOVED whole to `workspace/.refused/<home-name>-<UTC stamp>` (a rename on the same volume, never a copy-then-delete, never through a link: the folder is renamed by its own name, a link inside it stays a link and is never followed) and the app never reads, scans or lists that folder (not a walked home: material scan, `HomeFiles`, revisions all name homes from the chart). If the rename cannot be made, nothing is changed and the refusal stays, in words.
- Refuse, never guess; evidence stays true: the rebuild is `WorkspaceBuilder.Build` from the app's templates, and `AppFileManifest` records what it wrote afresh; material rows are untouched. Containment: refused while that position has an open launch (`TurnMeter.OpenAttemptIdFor(role)`), because a home is never moved from under a running seat.
