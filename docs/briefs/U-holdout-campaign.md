**The rule (stated in CONTRACTS; seat A decided, 2026-10-09):** a cutoff holds MARKET TIME for every pair (U-bar-holdout), so a campaign is about market time for every pair. (1) Its DEVELOPMENT months are its dataset's bars before the cutoff, `[first_bar, holdout_from)`, read from the ledger at
each look; a research run of ANY pair over ANY dataset whose bars' market span (`TapeHoldout.BarRefusal`'s, `TapeHoldout.cs:248-256`; an open side read as the run dataset's first or last bar, `Backtest.cs:851-877`) overlaps them is charged to that OPEN campaign — asked before the run,
registered in the run's transaction, its own campaign first then by id, any refusal rolling the run back. A fixture run charges nothing; a campaign over a fixture or REJECTED dataset is charged by its own dataset only; a feature's reach into the months is not counted, and CONTRACTS says so. (2)
A judgement reads `[the cutoff on its row, its dataset's window close)` (`Referee.cs:351-352`); a campaign's judgements spent are every verdict, of any pair, whose read span overlaps the span its own reads, its lineage's included; a second press over held months is TOLD and COUNTED, never
refused (a REJECTED dataset's window stays held, `TapeHoldout.cs:152-153`). (3) The class rides with the cutoff: while a dataset has an open campaign, a press that would change its class is refused in the owner's words, writing nothing; the same instant and class stays the Ok no-op. (4) A run
the app stops is refused: nothing recorded, nothing charged, no figures returned.
