**Where COUNCIL is silent, the choices — all in `CONTRACTS.md` as choices.** A holdout is a TIME CUTOFF on a dataset, UTC and INCLUSIVE, never a second dataset (a cutoff cannot be asked for by id, and one file stays hashed). A window is served only when its END is PROVED to be before the cutoff, so an **unbounded `to` is refused**: reading "no end" as "up to the cutoff" is clipping with extra steps. Moving a cutoff LATER is allowed and moving it EARLIER is refused, because only the second direction rewrites bars already served. The class and the cutoff are orthogonal — the cutoff decides what is served, the class decides what is charged. `HOLDOUT_WITHHELD` and `CAMPAIGN_BUDGET_REACHED` are new codes because their repairs differ from every existing one. Budgets are settings: **`CampaignTrialBudget` 200** and **`CampaignVerdictBudget` 3**, copied onto the campaign at open so a later nudge cannot move the standard under evidence already collected; zero means no attempts (the `AiDailyCostCap` reading) and `Unreadable()` falls to zero. Beyond the brief, and the one place I went further than it: **verdicts are counted over the whole renewal LINEAGE** rather than per campaign, because `COUNCIL.md`:132 says a renewal must not reset holdout access and a per-campaign count would hand it straight back. A renewal also carries the parent's POLICY text and sha, so more attempts never come with an easier standard.
