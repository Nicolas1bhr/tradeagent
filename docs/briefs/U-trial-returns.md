**The rule** (CONTRACTS "The backtest", a paragraph after :2689-2695; one sentence in "A trial"):
- Every run `Backtests.Record` records — charged, fixture or under no campaign — keeps a 1× and a 2× stream: `VenueFriction.Of(the dataset's venue)`'s fee and slippage (`VenueCostModel.cs:395-451`) × the multiple, the
  run's own increment and capital. 1× is the run's own trace when its model is exactly that; otherwise, and at 2×, the app evaluates again through `Backtest.Over` with the run's audience and window, its `BacktestRequest`
  equal to the run's but for `Model` (`Backtest.cs:254-271`) — else, or refused, that stream is recorded missing with why, the run as today. No venue, or no published fee: both missing, with why, and no day. An
  extra evaluation is the app's, served to no one: it is no `strategy_run` row and charges no trial (a trial is research's peek, `Database.cs:833-842`; what serving a stream costs is `U-experiments-op`'s to decide).
- A day is a UTC date; its mark is the `Equity` of the last `Bar` closing in it (a 00:00Z close ends the day before); its net return is mark ÷ previous mark − 1, the first from the declared capital. No closed bar:
  0 bars, no mark, no return — unknown, never 0 (`PRINCIPLES.md:27`) — and the next return runs from the last mark, naming its day (`since`). A flat day is a measured 0; a mark holds the open position, no exit fee.
- Written in `RecordRun`'s insert after the trades, behind :372, never updated or deleted; a stop, a refused `RegisterTrial` and any rollback leave none, and so does the referee's run. Read in-process only (`StreamsOf`,
  beside `TradesOf` :459; no stream is not a stream of no days); a read for agents is `U-experiments-op`'s, holding a stream back as `StrategyStore.Withheld` (:592-626) holds trades, a later cutoff included.
