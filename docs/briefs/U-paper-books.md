But everything a run's
orders pass through reads the ACCOUNT. The paper book keys a position by symbol alone (`PaperBook.cs:120-123`) on its one account, `PAPER-1` (`PaperConnector.cs:104`). A run's close (exit `ForwardRuns.cs:443-454`, stop and target `:590`,

An opener is charged the account's position (`:3599-3602`). So with two runs (a test writes `max_deployments` 2, `PaperDeploymentExitHygieneTests.cs:272-274`), A can rest no stop and can never exit while B holds. A's END sells
B's position as A's own, Research's note counts it (`:1481-1490`), and B's book stays long with no exit. One run already meets this beside a holding it did not open; CONTRACTS says so (`CONTRACTS.md:4144-4146`).
**The rule** (CONTRACTS "The paper deployment" and "The runner"; it replaces the NOT-claimed sentence at `:4144-4146`, and for runs the ceiling note at `:3795-3798`):
- A run's BOOK is its own `deployment_op` rows joined to the `fill` rows under their request ids: held, average cost, realised, known fees and fills with no fee. It is COMPLETE only when every request's `filled_quantity` (`Database.cs:170`) equals its ledger fills.
- A run's CLOSE must equal its own holding, and the account must hold at least that much. The END closes exactly the run's holding. A flat, complete book: "nothing to close". The account flat under a long, complete book,
  with no order of the run's still open: resolved, "closed outside the run". The account short of the book, or an incomplete book: owed, in words.
- A run's OPENER is charged its own holding + its version's open openers + the order. The envelope's standing paper allocations never sum past its ceilings. Every other order keeps the account readings.
**Observable result:** two runs on one symbol each rest their protection, exit and end with their own quantity, and the account holds their sum. Each run's line (Dashboard, `DailyReports.cs:395-396`, `deployment-list`) says what IT
holds, at what average, and what it has realised after costs. With one run per grant, as shipped, only two things change: that line, and an END that now leaves alone a holding the run did not open.
