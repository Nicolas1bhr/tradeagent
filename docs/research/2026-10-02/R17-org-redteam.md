## 0. Verdict

1. The shape is sound and mostly faithful to the owner: the chart is app data, code decides money and truth, the owner decides authority, managers
   decide from instruments with scored forecasts, cut rules are app events, and the watcher sits outside the chain. It does not secretly shrink the org.
2. As specified it cannot run on the owner's plan and cap: a GPT-6.1 Sol CLI turn reserves $3.20, larger than every default sub-envelope at $5 — the chief can never launch (#1).
3. It multiplies unconfined CLI seats that each hold the owner's ChatGPT login before containment, against FP § 25.4, COUNCIL's harness rule and R12's
   own O6, and § 14 omits credentials and untrusted reading (#2).
4. Order permission is bound to an id, but the chief can re-seat, re-occupy, re-parent and direct the one position that holds it (#3).
5. The watcher leaks through the design itself (the chief's scorecard, the Situation's day total) and, on plans, through the shared codex login, store and window (#4).
6. Both measures that run before any forward record — calibration and the chief's budget split — are the gameable ones: self-resolving forecasts and
   persuasion, which contradicts "narrative never moves budget" (#5, #6).
7. Sequencing breaks FP Law 10 (M0 now waits for four org units) and lets an inert lane-B unit block phase 0. M-org1 needs a unit scheduled after it, and the raw-item clock lost its slot (#7, #23–#25).
8. The owner is not told now that his plan buys about four strong-model managers, or that the organisation he described costs $20–50 a day; every route to
   hard-enforced org rules (API seats, Sign in with ChatGPT, a second vendor, contained execution) is a CARD in no wave (#8).
9. The audit line is the chief's staff — inside the chain it must check (#9).
10. All of it is fixable in the docs and the four lane-B briefs without changing the shape. In order: billing-class caps with envelope arithmetic; an owner ceiling on
    unconfined seats, with containment before growth; `operations` owner-only.

## 2. What the design gets right — keep it while fixing

- **The chart is app data with one writer.** No op writes it (`U-org-ledger` test (d)); titles, charters and messages grant nothing; ids are minted by the app.
- **`MayPlaceOrders` stays bound to the legacy id, never to headship,** with a headship mutant (`U-org-principals` item 4). The three silent
  collapses into the chair are named and refused (items 1, 2, 5). #3 extends this; it does not replace it.
- **The reservation tree is checked inside `Begin`'s one transaction,** with the sibling race red-first, two mutants, and LOST reservations kept in every ancestor (O4).
- **Trial and verdict keys carry no role,** so restructuring and replacement reset no evidence budget (L87-88, § 13.5).
- **R13's best-supported rules are adopted correctly:** decide from instruments, one accountable decider, no committees, adversarial briefs only at
  boundaries, deliveries forwarded by reference, retries in a fresh context, shallow trees, event-driven wakes.
- **Sub-organisations are cut by context** (team = family, division = information source; R13 D1), and breadth is reported as the effective number of
  independent teams rather than head-count (R14 § 3.1).
- **Cut rules are app events scaled to each version's volatility,** with no agent appeals and owner-only exceptions; live halving is an owner-confirmed reading (§ 16.4).
- **§ 14's enforced-versus-advisory table and the "unconfined seats" label** are the honesty pattern R10 asked for. Extend them (#2); do not soften them.
- **The watcher puts code detectors first,** has no authority, takes no paid turn on a quiet day, and has its own forecasts scored. System One runs only in shadow
  and only in the saving or cautious direction, never on the signal path; Jev reads public items only.
- **"AI proposes the ceiling" stays a record** the owner confirms with two presses, and the app sizes inside it (§ 12).
- **The plan limits are stated** (L218-224); they now need to reach the owner (#8).
- **The briefs keep the house discipline:** red-first tests, mutants, the legacy-pair guard (635 references), no schema change where none is needed, conflicts named.
