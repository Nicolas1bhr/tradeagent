FINDING 4 — MED — src/TradeAgent.Connectors.Atas/AtasConnector.cs:605 — Chunk completion is mistaken for byte progress, so a healthy slow reader is dropped as stalled

FINDING 8 — MED — src/TradeAgent.Gateway/TradingGateway.cs:635 — The blanket replay contract is implemented only for Place

`CancelAsync` and `ModifyAsync` authorize and resolve the target before consulting the idempotency store. A kill switch, paused health, or missing historical target can therefore prevent retrieval of an already-recorded result. `CloseAsync` re-authorizes and rereads positions before `PlaceAsync` checks the store; after a successful flatten, replay commonly returns `null` rather than the original `FILLED` request.

Check: complete cancel/modify/close calls, alter authorization or flatten state, repeat the same request ids, and assert byte-equivalent stored outcomes with no connector calls. Move durable replay lookup ahead of authorization, target resolution, and position reads for every mutating operation.

FINDING 9 — MED — src/TradeAgent.Gateway/GatewayPipeServer.cs:512 — Sweep uniqueness is limited to 32 bits and eventually collides with durable history

Check: inject a nonce seam returning the same value for two sweeps over different working orders and assert the second order is actually cancelled. A full 32-hex GUID still fits the 61-character budget.
